⚠ Read This First — Compliance Rules for This Product Category

Stündenglass and G Pen products are in a restricted category on Meta. The agency stays approved by framing ads as design objects and lifestyle products — not smoking accessories. Every piece of ad copy and every product title must follow these rules, or the account will be flagged.

✓ Always Do This

  • Ad account category: Home Decor & Design or Art & Collectibles
  • Product titles: "360° Gravity Infuser — Limited Edition" / "Precision Borosilicate Glass Device"
  • Ad copy: focus on design, engineering, craftsmanship, and collaboration story
  • Visuals: product beauty shots, unboxing, collab branding, lifestyle (no use shown)
  • Landing pages: open with product imagery and specs — not cannabis or smoke language

✗ Never Do This

  • Never use words: cannabis, weed, smoke, smoking, bong, hookah, hit, rip, sesh, 420 (in ad copy)
  • Never show anyone using the product to smoke in ad creative
  • Never categorize ad accounts under tobacco, smoking, or vaping
  • Never run ads that link to a page with cannabis-explicit language above the fold
  • Never use emojis that imply cannabis use (🌿💨🍃) in ad copy
