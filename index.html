Agency Execution Failure #3 of 3

$1,440 Burned in One Day.
No Kill Switch. No Alert.

February 20, 2026 — Stündenglass
0.11× ROAS

$1,440 in ad spend generated $160 in attributed revenue. A net loss of $1,285 in a single day. No campaign was paused. No alert was sent. The campaign ran from midnight to midnight at full budget.

What should have happened:

A ROAS floor of 0.8× checked at 10AM would have paused the campaign within the first 3 hours — limiting the damage to ~$150. This is a 15-minute setup in any ad management tool.

What This Reveals

The agency was operating on a weekly review cadence at minimum — not daily. Both the Feb 20 disaster and the 9-day high-ROAS scaling failure point to the same root cause: campaigns were reviewed in weekly batches, not monitored continuously.

The Fix Is Simple

Daily review. Automated ROAS floor. A human — or Claude — looking at the data every morning. None of these require sophisticated tools. They require attention.

Combined cost of all 3 failures: Pre-4/20 pullback (~$90–150K missed) + scaling failures (~$50–80K missed) + Feb 20 disaster ($1.3K lost) = $140,000–$230,000 in preventable revenue impact in 4 months.

Important Context for the Executive Team

Four Things to Understand
Before We Move Forward

01

The Numbers Here Are the Conservative Floor

All revenue figures are Triple Whale pixel-attributed — which runs 10–20% below Meta native ROAS due to iOS14 signal loss (Apple blocks Meta's cross-app tracking on ~65% of iPhones). Meta's own dashboard shows higher ROAS. The real performance is somewhere between the two. We're not overstating the problem — it may actually be even better than shown.

02

When We Leave the Agency, We Lose the Account History

All campaign data, machine learning, and audience signals live inside the agency's Business Manager — not ours. New accounts start from zero. Expect 3–4 weeks of lower-than-normal ROAS while the Meta algorithm re-learns our audiences. This is normal, unavoidable, and temporary. Budget for it.

03

The 4/20 2027 Clock Starts Now

4/20 is 350 days away. Starting the new accounts now gives us 11 months to build account health, ML training, and warm audiences before the next biggest sale of the year. Starting in Q1 2027 gives us weeks. Every month we delay is a month of compounding ML data we won't have on the biggest day of our calendar.

04

Request a Full Data Package From the Agency on Exit

Before or during the 30-day notice period: request all active campaign IDs, top audience segments, proven-compliant ad creative (any creative that has run successfully is compliance-proven — it has real value), and full account structure documentation. We own our Pixel data and Klaviyo lists regardless — those come with us automatically.

Risk Register

What Could Go Wrong
And How We Mitigate It

MED

New account ROAS underperforms for 3–4 weeks during learning period

Expected and normal. Meta's ML needs spend history to optimize. Run parallel with agency during this period — don't pull agency budget until new account ROAS stabilizes. Timeline: 3–4 weeks max.

MED

Ad creative fails review on fresh accounts

New accounts get stricter review than established ones. Mitigate: start with product catalog / Advantage+ Shopping format only (lowest rejection rate). Request proven-compliant creative from agency on exit — it's already been approved by Meta's systems.

MED

Primary ad account suspended

Pre-warmed backup accounts are live and ready. Recovery time: hours, not days. Three accounts per brand specifically for this reason. Same strategy the agency uses.

LOW

Agency non-cooperation on exit

We own Pixel data, customer lists (Klaviyo/Shopify), and Triple Whale attribution data. Campaigns can be rebuilt from scratch in ~2 days. Their cooperation on the data handoff is helpful but not required.

LOW

Meta MCP write-access not yet available on new accounts

If the official MCP integration isn't available on day one, Claude acts as the analysis and recommendation layer — a human implements the changes in Ads Manager. Same output, one extra step. Not a blocker.
