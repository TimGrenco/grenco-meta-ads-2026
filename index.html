1 primary + 2 warm backups. Fund all three. Run $10–20/day of brand content on backup accounts for 3 weeks to establish spend history before launching product campaigns.

03

Mirror exactly what the agency runs today. Catalog ads are the safest format for this product category — no "creative" to review, just product images and titles pulled from feed. Product titles: engineering/design language only.

04

Run new accounts at matching budget alongside agency accounts. Compare Triple Whale ROAS side-by-side. If within 10–20% for 5 consecutive days → migrate and give agency 30-day notice.

05
